# Bradcerb Brand Guidelines

## Who this is

Brad Erb. Public name: Bradcerb. Personal brand, not Triumph Tech and not Transform.

Published tagline: I build and teach AI work loops that turn recurring work into reliable, human-supervised systems.

## Voice

Clear, direct, warm. Short sentences. No filler. No hype.

Sound like a person who builds systems and still cares about the people in them.

Do not sound like a corporate deck, a growth-hack thread, or a Triumph Tech announcement.

## Visual system

Cream paper, pure ink, hard offset shadows, no blur.

- Paper / cream: `#fbf7ef`
- Ink: `#000000`
- Night: `#111111`
- Primary accent: Brand Orange `#f97316`
- Supporting: Brand Blue `#2563eb`, Brand Yellow `#ffdb32`, Brand Green `#22c55e`

Display type: Archivo Black. Body type: Inter.

## Logo

Logo files live in `logos/`. Until lockups are added, use the word "bradcerb" in Archivo Black, ink on cream or cream on night. Do not use Triumph marks, Magnus, or Transform marks here.

## What not to mix

Keep this kit personal. Do not pull Triumph Tech colors, type, mascot, or copy into personal work. Do not pull Transform product branding into this kit.
